Starting point: the policy defaults (±0.05 V around the 02b offset, 51 flux
points, 100 MHz span at 1 MHz, saturation at amplitude factor 0.02, joint
flux). Near the sweet spot the qubit only moves down, so if the parabola leaves
the window at the low end, move `frequency_shift_in_mhz` negative rather than
widening the span. The sweep must stay within ±0.4 V and below 1.99 x the z
`const` amplitude.

## Lesson — 2026-10-09, workflow aad09c89: never use `frequency_shift_in_mhz` != 0

- `03b_qubit_spectroscopy_vs_flux.py` plots on `freq_full` (which includes the
  shift) but fits peak positions on the unshifted detuning axis, so
  `drive_freq` / `idle_freq` / `sweetspot_freq` / the XY IF are all off by
  exactly -shift. Run `#382` (+50 MHz) put every red vertex marker 50 MHz below
  its parabola. q4 passed there; it was committed with +50 MHz added to
  idle/sweetspot/IF after an independent fit of `ds.h5` on `freq_full`
  confirmed the vertex (0.0514 V, 3.9254 GHz vs corrected node 3.9267 GHz).
  Widen `frequency_span_in_mhz` instead of shifting.
- After 02b moved q9/q10 by ~0.35 V, q3-q8 were off their sweet spots by
  +0.05 V (q3-q5) and -0.11..-0.13 V (q6, q7) — the default ±0.05 V window
  showed only steep slopes (`#381`). Joint-flux crosstalk from the q9/q10
  offset change is the likely cause. Sweet-spot frequencies were unchanged
  (q3 3.852, q4 3.926, q5 ~3.945, q6 4.200, q7 3.837 GHz), so 03a's lower values
  were simply off-sweet-spot readings.
- A qubit the analyzer passes is listed resolved even if its commit is
  withheld (q8 in `#381` fitted a faint flat q9/q10 feature, not its own
  line); it cannot be re-measured in the same node.
