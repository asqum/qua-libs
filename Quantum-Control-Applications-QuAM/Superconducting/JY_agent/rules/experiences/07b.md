> Current rule (operator decision 2026-10-14): 07b is measured once and always
> passes, so its rotation angle and threshold always commit and later nodes
> measure on I. Fidelity >= 0.80 with two Gaussian blobs only decides whether
> the qubit may use state discrimination and active reset (the thermal result
> then earns one active-reset repeat). Lessons below that fail or repeat 07b on
> fidelity or cloud shape are history; the shape lessons still explain why a
> qubit loses discrimination.
>
> Current reset rule: the accepted thermal-reset examples below remain valid 07b
> evidence, but they do not qualify active reset. Active reset is unlocked per
> qubit only by a later active-reset 07b repeat (earned by a thermal fidelity
> above 0.80 with two Gaussian blobs) that reaches normalized fidelity `>= 0.75`
> (`reset_policy.active_min_readout_fidelity`) with at most 15% of its shots on
> neither blob. State discrimination on any node still needs this workflow's
> 07b fidelity `>= 0.80` with two Gaussian blobs; other qubits use raw I/Q.
